GET INVOLVED

Partner with Darwin 1942 to bring this important Australian story to life ahead of the 85th anniversary of the Bombing of Darwin in February 2027.

Partner With Darwin 1942

AN OPPORTUNITY FOR YOUR RSL SUB BRANCH AND CLUB

This isn't a sponsorship proposal. This is an opportunity for your Sub Branch and Club to become the local custodian of the film within your region.

By partnering with the project, your RSL Sub Branch and Club will help bring this important Australian story to life throughout the 85th anniversary year of the bombing of Darwin in February 2027. Your support will contribute to the production, marketing and nationwide release of the feature documentary, ensuring this remarkable chapter of our history reaches communities across Australia.

What Your Sub Branch and Club Receives

PARTNERSHIP BENEFITS

Our partnership packages equip your RSL Sub Branch and Club with valuable resources to engage your community long after the credits roll, including:

  • Thousands of digital watch-at-home licences to share throughout your local community.
  • Educational resources and school screening opportunities that strengthen relationships with schools in your region.
  • Community Screening and engagement resources to promote your community events to local organisations including Lions, Probus, Men's Sheds, Women's Sheds and other community groups.
  • Exclusive initiatives designed to help raise your RSL Sub Branch and Club's profile, strengthen community engagement and support future membership growth.

BUILT ON EXPERIENCE

Darwin 1942 is being brought to life by an executive team with more than 40 years of combined experience in film production, distribution and audience engagement.

Together, we've helped connect powerful stories with communities across Australia and around the world.

OUR GUARANTEE

Darwin 1942 will be completed and released, regardless of any future partnership outcomes. The minimum viable budget required for this film has been secured.

Every partnership going forward will strengthen the project, increase the quality of the production, the number of Veteran stories we can include, and the scale of the reach and educational impact of the project.

We also guarantee the delivery of every partnership benefit outlined above, including your RSL Sub Branch and Club's recognition as the local custodian of the film, screening licences and community engagement resources.

When your RSL Sub Branch and Club partners with Darwin 1942, you're investing in a lasting legacy that will honour those who served and ensure their stories continue to inspire future generations.
