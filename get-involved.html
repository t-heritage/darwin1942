Partner with Darwin 1942 to bring this important Australian story to life ahead of the 85th anniversary of the Bombing of Darwin in February 2027.

Partner With Darwin 1942

AN OPPORTUNITY FOR YOUR RSL CLUB

This isn't a sponsorship proposal. This is an opportunity for your club to become the local custodian of the film within your region.

By partnering with the project, your RSL Club will help bring this important Australian story to life ahead of the 85th anniversary of the Bombing of Darwin in February 2027. Your support will contribute to the production, marketing and nationwide release of the feature documentary, ensuring this remarkable chapter of our history reaches communities across Australia.

What Your Club Receives

PARTNERSHIP BENEFITS

Our partnership packages equip your RSL Club with valuable resources to engage your community long after the credits roll, including:

  • Thousands of digital watch-at-home licences to share throughout your local community.
  • Educational resources and school screening opportunities that strengthen relationships with schools in your region.
  • Community screening and engagement resources for local organisations including Lions, Probus, Men's Sheds, Women's Sheds and other community groups.
  • Exclusive initiatives designed to help raise your club's profile, strengthen community engagement and support future membership growth.

BUILT ON EXPERIENCE

Darwin 1942 is being brought to life by two Australian organisations with more than 40 years of combined experience in film production, distribution and audience engagement. Together, we've helped connect powerful stories with communities across Australia and New Zealand.

Learn more about our team in the About the Film section.

OUR GUARANTEE

We are committed to delivering Darwin 1942. The film will be completed and released, regardless of any partnership outcomes. Every partnership strengthens the quality, reach and educational impact of the project, but its delivery is not dependent on any single partner outcome.

We also guarantee the delivery of every partnership benefit outlined in our proposal, including your club's recognition, screening licences and community engagement resources.

When your RSL Club partners with Darwin 1942, you're investing in a lasting legacy that will honour those who served and ensure their stories continue to inspire future generations.
