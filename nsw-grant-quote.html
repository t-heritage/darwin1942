GET A QUOTE FOR YOUR GRANT APPLICATION

We’ve put together a simple $1,200 + GST package to make it quick and easy for your Sub-Branch to apply for funding through the NSW Veteran Community Grants Program and take part in DARWIN 1942.

The package includes two public screening licences over a 12-month period, your own event page for each screening, and a personalised commemorative event kit. We’ll also provide the screening quote and suggested wording for your grant application.

The grant covers up to $3,000, so it can pay for the package and leave room for venue hire, catering and other event costs.

Fill in the form below to download a quote to attach to your grant application.
