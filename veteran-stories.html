COMMEMORATE A VETERAN STORY

Darwin 1942 is a place where Veterans, their families, friends, and RSL Sub Branches and Clubs can share their stories.

Tell us about the service and sacrifice of Veterans, share photos and videos of your parents and grandparents, share information from your RSL Sub Branch or Club.

Together, we will remember their service and sacrifice, and honour our Veterans.

"Without our Veterans, the Australia we live in today will be a totally, totally different place. We should never forget what the Veterans have done."
— Dr Norman Cramp, Director of the Darwin Military Museum
