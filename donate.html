Why Your Support Matters

HELP BRING THIS STORY TO AUSTRALIA

Darwin 1942 will be completed and released. The minimum viable budget for this film has been secured. But every contribution goes further — strengthening the quality of the production, increasing the number of Veteran stories we can include, and expanding the reach and educational impact of the project.

Your donation directly supports the production, distribution and creation of educational resources for schools, RSL Sub Branches and Clubs, and communities across Australia.

  • Fund veteran story collection and archival research
  • Support community screening programs across regional Australia
  • Develop educational resources for schools
  • Ensure the film reaches RSL Sub Branches and Clubs nationwide
  • Help preserve Australia's WW2 military history for future generations
