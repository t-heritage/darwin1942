More Than A Screening

COMMEMORATING SERVICE AND SACRIFICE

Darwin 1942 is more than a documentary. It is an opportunity to commemorate and share stories about one of the defining moments in our nation's history, and the Veterans involved.

As the 85th Anniversary of the bombing of Darwin approaches, this project creates meaningful ways for RSL Sub Branches and Clubs, museums and historical societies, schools, and communities to remember and honour the Veterans who defended Australia, while ensuring their stories continue to inspire future generations.

Every screening is more than a viewing. It becomes a conversation. A classroom. A remembrance service. A shared experience that strengthens the connection between generations and reminds us of the resilience, sacrifice, courage and the fighting spirit that helped shape modern Australia.

Leaving a Legacy Together

THE STORIES OF OUR VETERANS DESERVE MORE THAN PRESERVATION. THEY DESERVE PARTICIPATION.

Through community screenings, educational resources, local commemorative events and partnerships with RSL Sub Branches and Clubs, and community organisations across Australia, Darwin 1942 is designed to create a lasting legacy that extends far beyond the screen.

  • Honour the service and sacrifice of those who defended Australia.
  • Inspire younger generations through authentic Australian stories.
  • Support schools with meaningful educational resources.
  • Bring communities together around remembrance and commemoration.

Stories That Continue to Matter

KEEPING HISTORY ALIVE

Many of the Veterans who experienced the bombing of Darwin are no longer with us. Their voices, however, still have the power to educate, challenge and inspire.

By supporting Darwin 1942, you're helping ensure these stories remain part of Australia's living history — not simply preserved in archives, but experienced by communities, discussed in classrooms and shared around the country for generations to come.

Because remembrance is not simply about looking back. It's about shaping the future.
